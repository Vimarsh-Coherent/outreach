# Coherent Analytics — Product Pitch

## What we do
Coherent Analytics is a developer observability platform for engineering teams at mid-size B2B SaaS companies. We show why releases slow down and where bottlenecks happen across PRs, reviews, CI, and deployments.

## Ideal customer
- Engineering managers and VP Engineering
- 50–500 engineers
- Teams on GitHub, GitLab, or Azure DevOps
- Pain: long cycle times, unclear ownership of delays, spreadsheet-driven reporting

## Value propositions
1. **Release visibility** — See which PRs, reviews, or CI steps block each release.
2. **Team insights** — Compare cycle time across squads without manual work.
3. **Fast setup** — Connect your repo in under 15 minutes; no agent install.
4. **Slack alerts** — Notify when a release is at risk of missing its date.

## Proof points
- A 120-person fintech cut average release cycle from 12 days to 7 days in 8 weeks.
- Used by 40+ engineering teams in India and Southeast Asia.

Offer a 15-minute demo or a free 2-week trial for teams with 20+ engineers.

Friendly, professional, not pushy. Lead with their pain (slow releases, lack of visibility), not a feature dump.
