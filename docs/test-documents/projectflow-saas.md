# ProjectFlow — Outreach Brief

## Product summary
ProjectFlow is a lightweight project management tool for startup founders and small product teams (5–30 people). It replaces scattered Notion pages and Slack threads with one shared board for tasks, owners, and deadlines.

## Target audience
- Startup founders and COOs
- Early-stage product teams
- Companies that outgrew Trello but don't want Jira complexity

## Key benefits
- **Single source of truth** — Tasks, owners, due dates, and status in one view.
- **Async-friendly** — Built for remote teams; no meeting required to know what's blocked.
- **Integrations** — Slack notifications, Google Calendar sync, GitHub issue linking.

## Social proof
- 200+ startups onboarded in the last 12 months.
- Average team reports 30% fewer "what's the status?" messages in Slack after 4 weeks.
