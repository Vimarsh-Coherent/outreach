# FounderOS — Startup Operating System (Outreach Brief)

## Product summary
FounderOS is an all-in-one operating system for early-stage startup founders. It
combines goals (OKRs), weekly priorities, fundraising CRM, and an investor-update
generator in one workspace, so a founder can run the whole company from a single
Monday-morning view instead of juggling Notion, spreadsheets, and email.

## Who it's for
- Solo founders and founding teams at pre-seed to Series A startups (2–25 people)
- Founders raising a round who are tracking 50–150 investors
- Teams that outgrew a Notion template but find a full PM tool overkill

## Key modules
1. **Weekly Focus** — turns quarterly OKRs into the 3 priorities that matter this
   week, with an automatic Friday recap of what moved.
2. **Fundraising CRM** — a pipeline for investors with stages (researching →
   intro'd → pitched → diligence → committed), reminder nudges, and a warm-intro
   path finder across your team's LinkedIn connections.
3. **Investor Updates** — drafts a monthly update from your metrics and wins, in
   the format top seed funds expect (KPIs, asks, lowlights), ready to send in
   minutes.
4. **Runway tracker** — live cash-out date from your burn, with scenario sliders
   for hiring and revenue.
5. **Founder inbox** — one place for tasks, intros owed, and follow-ups, so nothing
   promised in a meeting gets dropped.

## Quantified value
- Founders save an average of 6 hours/week on operational busywork.
- Monthly investor updates go out on time for 90% of users vs. 40% before.
- Teams using Weekly Focus report shipping their top priority 2x more often.
- Setup takes 15 minutes; import OKRs and your investor list from a spreadsheet.
